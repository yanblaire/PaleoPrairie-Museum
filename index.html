Our Goal

To educate, engage, and inspire curiosity about the prehistoric life of western Kansas. We want visitors to learn about our digs, see fossil preparation in action, and explore our collection. We also aim to raise funds for future digs and preservation efforts.

About Us

Our museum began with a simple but powerful idea: to keep the fossils of western Kansas in western Kansas. Nestled in a region rich with prehistoric history, we are committed to uncovering, preserving, and showcasing the ancient life that once roamed our land. It all started when a local dig site uncovered a nearly complete fossilized skeleton of a marine reptile, and rather than see it shipped out of state, a small team of paleontology enthusiasts and community members worked together to find a way to preserve it locally. From that effort grew a mission to support fossil digs in western Kansas and build a place where discoveries stay rooted in the land they were found. Today, our museum proudly funds archaeological digs, preserves rare fossils, and tells the story of Kansas’ ancient life—from the inland sea to the towering dinosaurs that left their mark here millions of years ago.

Current Exhibits

Sea Monsters of the Kansas Inland Sea

Step back in time 80 million years when western Kansas was underwater. Explore life in the Western Interior Seaway with fossil specimens of mosasaurs, plesiosaurs, and giant ammonites. Interactive displays show how these marine reptiles hunted, lived, and ruled the ancient sea.

Highlight Fossils:

  • Tylosaurus skull
  • Plesiosaur flipper bones
  • Ammonite spiral shells

Prairie Giants: Dinosaurs of the High Plains

Discover the land-dwelling dinosaurs that roamed Kansas when the waters receded. Learn about the climate and vegetation that supported these animals, and see full skeletal reconstructions of local species.

Highlight Fossils:

  • Niobrarasaurus coleii (an armored dinosaur unique to Kansas)
  • Hadrosaur limb bones
  • Fossilized dinosaur tracks and coprolites (dino poop!)

The Dig: Unearthing the Past

Go behind the scenes of a real paleontological excavation in western Kansas. This exhibit features tools, photos, and interactive stations where guests can simulate digging and preparing fossils just like our field teams do.

Special Features:

  • Real fossils from recent digs
  • Time-lapse of fossil cleaning and preservation
  • Volunteer shout-outs and stories from the field
